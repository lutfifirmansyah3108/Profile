👨 I'm a postdoctoral fellow in the Saudi's top University.

<p align="justify">
I am Mochamad Lutfi Firmansyah, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Refining and Advanced Chemicals, **King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals** (KFUPM), Saudi Arabia. My work sits at the intersection of materials chemistry, environmental engineering, and sustainable resource management. Over the past decade, my research has evolved into two major themes,  _resource recovery and recycling of precious and critical metals_ and _development of advanced materials for the removal of emerging pollutants from water systems_.
</p>
<p align="justify">
My academic journey spans three countries and three degrees. I completed my Bachelor of Science at Institut Teknologi Bandung, Indonesia 🇮🇩, where I first explored zeolite-based mesoporous materials. I then pursued a Master of Philosophy at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia 🇲🇾, working on mesoporous materials and fixed-bed catalysis, before earning my Doctor of Engineering from Kyushu University, Japan 🇯🇵, specializing in solvent extraction, metal recycling, and membrane transport. Prior to my current postdoctoral appointment, I served as a Lecturer in Nanotechnology Engineering at Universitas Airlangga, Indonesia, where I taught courses ranging from Basic Chemistry and Spectroscopy to Material Characterization and Nanotechnology.
</p>
<p align="justify">
Whether through rigorous laboratory research, academic teaching, or community science engagement, my goal remains the same: to develop practical, sustainable solutions for the resource and environmental challenges of our time.
</p>
