👨 I'm a postdoctoral fellow in the Saudi's top University.

I am Mochamad Lutfi Firmansyah, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Interdisciplinary Research Center for Refining and Advanced Chemicals, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM), Saudi Arabia. My work sits at the intersection of materials chemistry, environmental engineering, and sustainable resource management — with a particular focus on developing low-cost, highly functionalized adsorbents for wastewater treatment and precious metal recovery. Over the past decade, my research has evolved into two major themes. The first is the recovery and recycling of precious and critical metals and development of advanced materials for the removal of emerging pollutants from water systems.
My academic journey spans three countries and three degrees. I completed my Bachelor of Science at Institut Teknologi Bandung, Indonesia, where I first explored zeolite-based mesoporous materials. I then pursued a Master of Philosophy at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, working on mesoporous materials and fixed-bed catalysis, before earning my Doctor of Engineering from Kyushu University, Japan, specializing in solvent extraction, metal recycling, and membrane transport.
Prior to my current postdoctoral appointment, I served as a Lecturer in Nanotechnology Engineering at Universitas Airlangga, Indonesia, where I taught courses ranging from Basic Chemistry and Spectroscopy to Material Characterization and Nanotechnology. I am also a published author of two Indonesian-language books on nanotechnology aimed at public audiences, and the creator of Ayo Eksperimen, a science outreach YouTube channel for children.
Whether through rigorous laboratory research, academic teaching, or community science engagement, my goal remains the same: to develop practical, sustainable solutions for the resource and environmental challenges of our time.
